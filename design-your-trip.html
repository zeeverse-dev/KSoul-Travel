How it works

Five steps to your
dream journey

01Share Your Vision
STEP 01

Share Your Vision

Fill out the questionnaire and tell me about your dream trip, travel style, preferences, expectations, special requests, and every detail that matters to you.

STEP 02

Travel Review

I carefully review your request to fully understand what you're looking for and create a personalized proposal tailored to your vision.

STEP 03

Your Approval

Review the proposal and let me know if you'd like any adjustments. Once you're happy with every detail, we'll move forward with the final planning process.

STEP 04

Planning

Once approved, I take care of the complete organization of your trip from A to Z, carefully managing every detail to create an experience designed entirely around you.

STEP 05

Enjoy Your Journey

From the planning stage until your return home, I remain available throughout your journey to provide support whenever needed, so you can simply relax and enjoy the experience.
